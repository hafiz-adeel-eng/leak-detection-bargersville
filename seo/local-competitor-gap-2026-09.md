# Nearby towns: keyword and competitor gap review (September 2026)

New town pages (all within 20 km of Bargersville): Southport & Perry Township (~16 km), Morgantown (~18 km), Nineveh & Prince's Lakes (~20 km). Mooresville, Beech Grove, Martinsville and Edinburgh (20–25 km) were researched but left out to keep the service area within 20 km.

## Search demand (Google Ads Keyword Planner, Indiana, last 12 months)

- "leak detection {town}" and "slab leak {town}" terms: under 10 searches a month each (Planner reports them as 10 or no data).
- "plumbers in mooresville indiana": 90 a month; "plumber mooresville in" / "plumber martinsville in": 10.
- "slab leak detection indianapolis", "water leak detection indianapolis", "pool leak detection indianapolis": about 10 each.

Low volume, but high intent and low competition: the page that answers the question best for the town tends to win.

## Who ranks now and what they miss

| Ranking page type | Examples | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Regional leak company town pages | Indiana Leak Detection (indianaleak.com/area-we-serve/...) | Short and generic; mixes gas, sewer and water; no ZIPs, water utility, local causes, costs or FAQs |
| Directory / lead-gen templates | corkd.com, out-of-state "{town}-in" subdomains | Same text for every town (the Mooresville IN and Mooresville NC pages are nearly identical); no local facts |
| Plumbers with a leak detection service page | Hudson Plumbing (Martinsville), Dutton, Mister Quik | Repair-focused; little on how the leak is located, service line ownership or what to check first |
| Directories | Yelp, BBB | Lists only |

## How the new pages fill the gaps

- Real local facts with sources: 2020 Census population, ZIP codes, county, and the actual water provider (Morgantown Water Utility, Prince's Lakes utility, Citizens Water).
- Who owns the service line, and when to call the utility first.
- Housing and leak causes specific to the town: long rural lines and wells (Morgantown), seasonal lake homes (Nineveh & Prince's Lakes), mid-century slab homes (Southport & Perry Township).
- Meter test steps, cost factors, local FAQs, and links into the matching guide cluster (well pump, galvanized, pinhole, frozen pipes, water line cost).
- Schema: Service with City (sameAs, geo, county), WebPage, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList.
