# Nationwide city pages: keyword and competition research (September 2026)

25 city pages at `/leak-detection/<city-st>/`. Calls go to one tracking number and are answered through a network of independent local leak detection and plumbing pros. Each page says so.

## How the cities were picked

1. About 90 mid-size cities in slab-heavy and hard-water states were checked in Google Ads Keyword Planner (US, last 12 months) for "leak detection {city} {st}".
2. Cities with measurable demand (20+ searches a month) were kept.
3. The top Google results were reviewed for each city. Cities where results are mostly general plumbers, directories or a regional franchise sub-page score better than cities with a strong dedicated local specialist.
4. Local facts on each page were checked: 2020 Census population, water utility, and soil, water hardness and climate factors, with sources.

## Page structure

- H1: "Leak Detection in {City}, {ST}".
- H2s that each carry a keyword with the city name: slab leak detection and repair, water line leak detection, hidden leak detection, pool leak detection (where pools are common), how to check for a leak, leak detection cost, FAQs.
- Unique local section: why leaks happen in that city (soil, water hardness, freezes, sinkholes, hurricanes, housing age), plus the water utility and service line ownership.
- Schema: Service with City (sameAs, geo, county), WebPage, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
- Phone number in the hero, an inline CTA, the sidebar, the CTA band, the header, the mobile call bar and a floating "Contact Us" button (the phone icon shakes; turned off for users who prefer reduced motion).

## Risks

- Google treats large sets of near-identical city pages as doorway pages. These pages share a template, so the local sections must stay genuinely unique. Add real job photos, reviews or case studies per city when available.
- The brand name and logo say Bargersville / Johnson County. For out-of-state visitors that can reduce trust; consider a neutral brand or a separate domain if this network grows.
