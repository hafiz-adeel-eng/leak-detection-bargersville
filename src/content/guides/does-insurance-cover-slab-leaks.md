A slab leak raises two questions at once: how do I fix it, and who pays for it? Insurance coverage depends on your policy, but most policies follow the same general pattern. This guide explains it so you know what to ask.

> **This is general information, not insurance advice.** Your policy's exact wording decides what's covered. Talk to your agent or insurer.

## The three parts of a slab leak claim

| Part | What it means | Commonly… |
|---|---|---|
| **Water damage** | Wet flooring, baseboards, drywall, cabinets | Covered when the damage is sudden and accidental |
| **Access / tear-out** | Opening the slab and flooring to reach the pipe, then putting it back | Often covered |
| **The pipe itself** | Repairing or replacing the worn or failed pipe | Often **not** covered |

Some policies also offer optional endorsements, such as service line coverage for buried lines. Check whether yours has one.

## What's often excluded

- **Gradual or long-term leaks** — damage that built up over weeks or months
- **Wear and tear, corrosion and age** of the pipe
- **Mold** that results from a leak that wasn't addressed
- **Foundation movement** unrelated to a covered water event

This is why acting early matters. If you notice [the signs of a slab leak](/guides/how-to-find-a-slab-leak/) — a warm floor, the sound of running water, a meter that keeps moving — get it confirmed and documented.

## How to make a claim go smoothly

1. **Stop the water.** Close the main shutoff, and turn off the water heater on a hot-side leak. See [what to do in a water leak emergency](/guides/water-leak-emergency-what-to-do/).
2. **Document first.** Photos and video of the damage before cleanup.
3. **Call your insurer early.** Ask what's covered and whether they want an adjuster to see the damage before repairs.
4. **Get the leak located and documented.** A report with the marked location, photos and readings shows where the water came from.
5. **Keep every receipt** — detection, drying, repairs and anything you replace.
6. **Keep your water bills** that show the jump in usage.

## Where leak detection helps

Insurers want to know **where** the leak is and **what caused** the damage. Professional slab leak detection gives you:

- the exact location, marked on the floor,
- photos and readings from the testing, and
- a smaller repair opening, which keeps access and restoration costs down.

After the leak is found, your plumber can recommend a [spot repair, reroute or repipe](/guides/slab-leak-repair-options/).
